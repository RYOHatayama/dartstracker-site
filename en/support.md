## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q. Can I carry my data over to a new device?

A. Data is currently stored on your device only. A backup feature for device transfers is planned for a future update.

### Q. How do I cancel my subscription?

A. You can cancel from **Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions** on iOS.

### Q. What about refunds?

A. Refunds for subscriptions can be requested through Apple Support:
[https://reportaproblem.apple.com/](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/)
